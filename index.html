Smart Recruiter - Hiring, reimagined with AI.

A recruitment platform designed to reduce repetitive work for recruiters while creating a clear, guided experience for candidates.

Role
Product designer
Year
25-2026
Timeline
16 weeks
Team
Manager, Team Mates, Developers
"Hiring should feel simpler. AI should do the heavy lifting."

That was the direction that came out of our stakeholder discussions while working on Smart Recruiter. The platform had to support a complete hiring journey, from creating jobs and managing candidates to conducting interviews and evaluating them, without making the experience feel complex.

The challenge was finding the right places for AI to actually help. We discussed how AI could reduce repetitive work for recruiters, while still keeping them in control of important decisions.

That led us to focus on a few key areas: AI-powered job creation, AI interviews, and bringing interview insights together within the candidate profile so recruiters could review and compare candidates more easily.

On the candidate side, the focus was different: making the application and interview experience feel simple, clear, and guided.

The Challenge

When we started designing Smart Recruiter, the goal was simple: make the hiring process faster and easier with AI. But the recruitment journey involved several complex steps:

  • Creating and setting up a job required manual effort.
  • Recruiters had to manage candidates and interviews across different stages.
  • Reviewing interview results and comparing candidates could become time-consuming.
  • Candidates also needed a simple and guided experience from application to interview.
For recruiters, the process could feel time-consuming. For candidates, the experience needed to feel simple and clear. The challenge was finding the right places for AI to reduce effort without making the hiring experience more complicated.

Saumya Sinha - Talent Acquisition Specialist

  • "Creating & managing candidates takes too much time."
  • "I need an easier way to review interview results."

Aditya Singh - Software Engineer

  • "The interview process should feel simple and guided."
  • "I want to know what happens at each step."
The Problem

Smart Recruiter had the potential to simplify hiring, but the process involved multiple complex workflows across job creation, candidate management, interviews, and evaluation. Recruiters needed to spend time on repetitive tasks, while candidates needed a more guided and straightforward experience.

Why this problem mattered for Smart Recruiter?
  1. Hiring involves multiple users and stages, making consistency and simplicity critical.
  2. AI was a key part of the product, but it needed to solve real problems rather than add complexity.
  3. Making recruitment faster and easier for both recruiters and candidates was central to creating a better hiring experience.
Screens
01 — AI-Powered Job Creation

Recruiters often spend time manually creating job descriptions and defining requirements. I introduced AI to help generate the initial job setup from basic inputs, while keeping the recruiter in control to review and edit the output.

The experience was designed to make AI feel like an assistant rather than another complex step in the workflow.

02 — AI Interview

The interview experience needed to work for candidates without making AI feel intimidating. I designed a guided flow that prepares the candidate, checks their system, and clearly communicates what is happening before the interview begins.

The AI then conducts the interview while keeping the interaction focused and easy to follow.

03 — Candidate Profile & Evaluation

After an interview, recruiters need to quickly understand how a candidate performed without going through information scattered across different stages. I brought the candidate's key information and interview evaluation together in one place.

This gives recruiters a clearer view of the candidate and makes it easier to review interview performance and make hiring decisions.

AI wasn't added everywhere. I focused on the moments where it could reduce repetitive work, guide users through complex tasks, and help recruiters make better decisions.
What changed?
  1. Faster job creation → AI reduced repetitive work for recruiters.
  2. Simpler interviews → Candidates could complete AI-led interviews through a more guided experience.
  3. Better candidate evaluation → Interview insights were organized in one place, making candidates easier to review and compare.
  4. More connected hiring → Recruiters and candidates could move through the hiring journey within one platform.
Reflection

This project taught me that AI design isn't about adding AI everywhere, it's about using it where it genuinely helps.

Working on Smart Recruiter helped me understand how to design AI experiences that feel useful without taking control away from the user. From AI-powered job creation to AI interviews and candidate evaluation, the focus was always on reducing effort and making complex processes easier.

It also taught me to think about both sides of a product, making the recruiter's work more efficient while keeping the candidate experience simple and guided. Most importantly, I learned that good AI design should feel helpful, clear, and natural, not intimidating.

Qubit V01 - Simplifying complex insurance operations.

An insurance CRM built for brokers, admins, and accountants to manage the complete client lifecycle, from policies, to documents, tasks, and accounting, all within one connected workspace.

Role
UI/UX designer
Year
24-2025
Timeline
34 weeks
Team
Founder, Developers
The Challenge

The client already had a working tool, but managing insurance operations meant juggling between multiple systems. Client information lived in one place, documents were managed through tools like Google Drive or Dropbox, financial work happened separately, and different teams had their own ways of managing tasks.

"We want our teams to manage the entire client journey from one place. Right now, they have to switch between different systems for policies, documents, tasks, and accounting. We need a CRM that brings everything together while keeping the experience simple for our teams."
What we heard from the team!
Too many systems
Teams had to switch between different tools to complete one workflow.
Complex information
A single client could have policies, claims, documents, tasks, and financial records connected to them.
Different users, different needs
Brokers, admins, and accountants worked differently but needed to use the same platform.
An older user base
The product needed to be simple and familiar while still being powerful enough to handle complex insurance operations.
Bringing the workflow together

The goal was to create one connected workspace around the entire client lifecycle, instead of making teams switch between different systems for different tasks.

We organized the product around key workflows including Clients, Policies, Quotes, Claims, Tasks, Activities, Notes, and Files, with a dedicated Accounting module covering invoices, payments received, payments made, general ledger, and reconciliation.

The navigation brought these interconnected workflows into one place, giving users a clear way to move from a client and their policies to related claims, documents, tasks, and financial information.

Designing around the users

A big part of the project was understanding how the CRM was actually being used before changing the experience. Since Qubit already had an existing system, I looked beyond stakeholder requirements and studied real user behaviour, existing workflows, and the tools teams were using alongside the CRM.

How we understood the users
Stakeholder Discussions
We spoke with the client and internal stakeholders to understand business goals, user responsibilities, and pain points.
User Behaviour
We used session-behaviour analysis to observe how users navigated the existing product, where they clicked, what they used frequently, and where they experienced friction.
Existing Workflows
We looked at how teams managed information across the CRM and other tools, helping us identify where workflows were fragmented or duplicated.
Role-Based Access
We mapped the different responsibilities of brokers, admins, and accountants to understand what each role actually needed from the platform.

Broker

  • "I need a quick view of my clients and everything related to them."

Admin

  • "I need to keep everything organized and make sure nothing gets missed."

Accountant

  • "I need all the financial information connected and easy to track."
What I focused on as a designer

Throughout the project, my focus was less on adding more features and more on making a complex system easier to understand.

I worked on end to end process, including:

  • Information architecture
  • Navigation and nesting
  • Role-based experiences
  • Complex workflows
  • UI and reusable components
  • Design system consistency
The hardest part — Accounting

The Accounting module was the most challenging part of the CRM because it was a completely new domain for me, beyond insurance itself. Before designing it, I had to understand how accountants worked and how each financial activity connected.

The biggest challenge was understanding these connections and turning them into a workflow that felt natural for accountants while remaining simple enough for admins without deep financial knowledge.

Impact

The platform is currently being used by 60+ agents, with 98% user satisfaction across the existing workflow. As the product continues to evolve from an insurance tool into a scalable SaaS platform, it is being prepared to support multiple brokers and insurance teams worldwide, making the work an important foundation for its next stage of growth.

"Qubit taught me that good enterprise design isn't about simplifying the business itself, but making complex business processes feel simple for the people using them."
